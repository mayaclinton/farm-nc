🌿 Introducing FARM-NC

By Maya Clinton July 24, 2025

We’re excited to launch FARM-NC, an EPA-funded research project building a framework for Whole-Farm Natural Capital Accounting, co-developed with farmers, for farmers.

Our Aim

To support sustainable farming by mapping, measuring, and valuing the natural capital of Irish farms from carbon and water to biodiversity and resilience.

Key Objectives

  • Boost environmental outcomes
  • Support climate resilience
  • Inform national policy with real-world data

Collaboration & Leadership

FARM-NC brings together hydrology, ecology, remote sensing, economics, and systems modelling to build tools that are both scientifically rigorous and practically useful.

Led by Dr. Jimmy O'Keeffe at DCU, our team includes researchers from DCU and Trinity College Dublin, each bringing cross-disciplinary expertise in flood risk, ecosystem services, and sustainable land use.
